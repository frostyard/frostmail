# Plan 0007: M4.5 people, calendar and tasks

M4.5 adds contacts, calendars and a task list to Frostmail, integrated with
mail in one window as Outlook's client was: People with contact cards in
mail, Calendar with invitations answered from the reader, Tasks with
Google Tasks and flagged mail, a To-Do bar beside the inbox, and reminders
that reach the user whether or not the app is open. It runs while
[M4](0006-m4-daily-driver.md)'s seven-day trial runs, and comes before M5
(Mail.app parity) in the [roadmap](0002-roadmap.md). Editing events is the
milestone after this one; answering invitations is in this one.

## Decisions taken for M4.5

- [ADR-0017](../adr/0017-sync-contacts-calendars-and-tasks.md): CardDAV and
  CalDAV for every account, Google Tasks for Gmail's tasks, flagged mail as
  tasks; services per account, off until turned on; `davx` over a small
  go-webdav fork; polling every 5 minutes and on focus.
- [ADR-0018](../adr/0018-keep-contacts-events-and-tasks-as-sent.md): sources
  stored as sent, edits as patches with conditional writes, occurrences
  materialized into a window, IANA time.
- [ADR-0019](../adr/0019-answer-invitations-through-the-server.md):
  invitations answered through the server's scheduling, else by iTIP mail.
- [ADR-0020](../adr/0020-one-window-for-mail-calendar-people-and-tasks.md):
  one window with four modules, a To-Do bar, contact cards, a reminder
  window.
- [ADR-0021](../adr/0021-run-task-cards-with-codex.md): Codex runs the task
  cards, which may be larger (M and L); the planner writes their contracts
  and reviews each before it merges.
- [design/pim.md](../design/pim.md): the mechanism.
- **The trial stays safe.** The user's installed maild and app come from the
  `m4-trial` branch (main as of the M4 trial's start) until the trial ends;
  trial fixes land on `main` and are cherry-picked there. M4.5's
  migrations are additive and its services are off per account, so
  installing it later changes no mail.

## The user's setup (needed by Phase 5)

1. In the Google Cloud project of the user's OAuth client: enable the
   CalDAV API, the Google Calendar API, the People API (for CardDAV) and the
   Tasks API, and add the scopes `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar`,
   `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/carddav` and
   `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/tasks` to the consent screen.
2. Sign the Gmail account in again from Settings when its services are
   turned on. iCloud needs nothing new: the app-specific password covers
   CalDAV and CardDAV.

## Phase 1 — Contracts and foundations (planner)

- This plan, ADRs 0017–0020, design/pim.md, specs/pim-ui.md (the four

- Migration 0007: services, collections, objects, contacts, people,
  events, attendees, instances, alarms, reminders, tasks.
- `third_party/go-webdav` fork: CalDAV sync-collection, conditional raw
  `PUT` and `DELETE`, recorded in `FROSTMAIL-PATCHES.md`.

  `internal/gtasks`, `internal/calendar`, `internal/vcardx`, `internal/itip`,
  `internal/davtest`; `tools/zonesgen` with the pinned CLDR table.
- The cards below, with stubs and given tests, each checked against a
  reference implementation.
- **Done when:** `make check` and `make ui-check` are green, the fork's
  tests pass, and Phase 2's cards are ready (later phases' cards are written
  as their phases start).

## Cards (Codex, [ADR-0021](../adr/0021-run-task-cards-with-codex.md))

Each card has a fixed file list, a contract and given tests from the
planner, runs with `make task T=NNNN`, and is reviewed before it merges.

| Card | Size | Phase | What |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `internal/vcardx` | M | 2 | vCard 3 and 4 into contact fields; golden tests over Google, iCloud and Nextcloud cards |
| CardDAV sync | L | 2 | the DAV loop for address books in `internal/mailsync` over `davx` and `davtest`: discovery, sync-collection, multiget, the objects and contacts stores |
| People | M | 2 | people linked by email, autocomplete ranking, `people.card` |
| People module | L | 2 | the module switcher, the people list and contact pane, the contact card popover |
| Occurrences | L | 3 | `internal/calendar`: rrule-go expansion with overrides, the instances window, zones (with `tools/zonesgen`), `calendar.range` |
| CalDAV sync | M | 3 | calendars and events, attendees, instances refreshed on change |
| Calendar module | L | 3 | day, week and month grids, the small month, the To-Do bar |
| Reminders | M | 3 | next alarm per account in maild, the reminder window |
| Google Tasks | L | 4 | `internal/gtasks`, the Tasks loop, task ops through `pending_ops` |
| Tasks module | L | 4 | lists, subtasks, due dates, flagged mail, completing a flag |
| Invitations | L | 4 | `internal/itip` (`REQUEST` in, `REPLY` out, golden tests), `calendar.invitation`, the invitation card |
| `mailctl` | M | any | `people ls/show`, `cal ls/agenda`, `tasks ls/add/done` |

The planner keeps what [ADR-0021](../adr/0021-run-task-cards-with-codex.md)
reserves: schemas and migrations, the go-webdav patches, `davtest`'s
servers, the OAuth scopes and sign-in, and `calendar.respond`, which
decides what is sent and to whom (ADR-0019).

## Phase 2 — People

- Cards: `internal/vcardx`, CardDAV sync, People, People module.
- Planner: Settings' services toggles with discovery, Add to Contacts
  (`dav.put` with `If-None-Match: *`), the per-service verify check.
- **Done when:** a Radicale address book and the in-process server sync
  into People both ways for a new contact, and verify compares them clean.

## Phase 3 — Calendar

- Cards: Occurrences, CalDAV sync, Calendar module, Reminders.
- Planner: reminders as desktop notifications with Snooze and Dismiss when
  the app is closed (`internal/notify`).
- **Done when:** a recurring series with an override, an all-day event, an
  event in a Windows-named zone and one across a DST change show at the
  right times in every view and remind on time, with the app open and
  closed.

## Phase 4 — Tasks and invitations

- Cards: Google Tasks, Tasks module, Invitations.
- Planner: `calendar.respond` by server scheduling or iTIP mail through the
  outbox, updates and cancellations, read-only accounts.
- **Done when:** a task ticked in Frostmail is done in Google Tasks and the
  reverse; an invitation from the test server's second user is answered
  from the reader and the organizer receives exactly one reply.

## Phase 5 — On the user's accounts

- The throwaway Gmail account first: contacts, calendars and tasks on,
  signed in again with the new scopes; recordings become replay tests.
- Then the user's real Gmail and iCloud, read-only first, as in M4.
- Invitations both ways between the throwaway and real accounts: Google's
  and iCloud's scheduling verified and recorded in their profiles.
- **Done when:** the user's contacts, calendars and Google Tasks are in
  Frostmail; verify is clean for every service; an invitation sent to each
  account is answered from Frostmail with exactly one reply; reminders
  fired on time with the app closed for a day.

## Later / ideas

- Event editing: create, move, resize, invite, recurring edits (this, this
  and following, all): the next milestone.
- Free/busy and a scheduling assistant; proposing new times (`COUNTER`).
- Google's other contacts through the People API; editing contacts.
- Google Calendar API adapter (Meet links, working location, focus time).
- Calendar in its own window; two calendars side by side.

## Open questions

- **Do Google and iCloud send replies for an answer made over CalDAV?**
  Decided by Phase 5's invitations; recorded in the provider profiles.
- **Are Google's default reminders visible over CalDAV as `VALARM`s?**
  Decided by Phase 5; if not, Frostmail applies the calendar's default
  reminder itself.
