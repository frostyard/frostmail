# T-0127: Mute in the list's menu and the muted bell

## Goal

Mute a conversation, as in Mail.app: its new mail never notifies, and its
rows show a crossed-out bell.

## Read first

- `docs/specs/ui.md`: "Line 1" (the bell) and "Context menu" (Mute);
  `docs/design/organize.md`: "Mute and block".
- `app/src/rpc/gen/api.ts`: `message.mute`, `MessageSummary.muted`.
- `app/src/app/ListContainer.tsx` (`menuItems`, `onMenuSelect`) and
  `app/src/features/list/MessageRow.tsx` (the alarm-clock glyph is the
  model for the bell).
- The given tests; `docs/tasks/EXECUTOR.md`.

## Contract

- **`MessageRow`**: when `message.muted`, a `span` with `role="img"`,
  `aria-label` and `title` "Muted", classed `shrink-0` and the row's
  secondary color, holding Lucide `BellOff` at 12px (`aria-hidden`),
  first in line 1's right-hand glyphs (before the paperclip).
- **`ListContainer`**: after Mark as Read or Mark as Unread, before Remind
  Me, an item "Mute", or "Unmute" when every loaded message of the
  menu's set is muted; disabled on a read-only account. It calls
  `message.mute {ids: the menu's ids, muted}`, `muted` true for Mute and
  false for Unmute; a failure only logs.

## Tests (given, do not edit)

`MessageRow.mute.test.tsx`, `Mute.test.tsx`, and `ListMenu.test.tsx`
(T-0126's with Mute). Every other app test must keep passing,
`ListMenu.rules.test.tsx` and `RemindMe.test.tsx` included.

## Out of scope

Shortcuts, maild, the mock, and every file not under `touch`.
